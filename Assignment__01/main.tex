    \begin{exercise}{2}
    \hfill

    BLAZER: color 1, color 2, color 3
    
    PANTS: color 1, color 2, color 3
    
    SHIRTS: 2 white, 1 pink, 1 purple, 1 black
    
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $3/5$ - chance to choice the right colored shirt (2 white + 1 black)
        \item $1$ - whichever is chosen first blazer or pants the color doesn't matter
        \item $1/3$ - chance to pick the same color as either the blazer or pants that is already chosen
    \end{itemize}

    $ = \frac{3}{5} \times 1 \times \frac{1}{3}$

    $ = \frac{3}{15}$
    
    $= \frac{1}{5}$
    
    Bill has a 1/5 chances that he makes a good impression.

    OR

    Total number of combinations: $3 \times 3 \times 5 = 45$

    Number of good impression outfits: $3 \times 3 = 9$

    $\frac{number of good impression outfits}{total number of combinations} = \frac{9}{45} = \frac{1}{5}$

    Bill has a 1/5 chances that he makes a good impression.
    \end{exercise}
